· Bugs — Fix First ·

4 Things Currently Wrong

Bug 01
Overdue milestones still show as "Upcoming"
UKISET Exam Prep Start was due 1 Feb 2026 — it is 35 days overdue. The dot is blue and says "🔵 Upcoming · High Priority." We calculate the day count correctly (-35 days) but ignore it for the visual status. A parent sees conflicting information.
Fix: calculate overdue/upcoming from date, not Notion status field. Auto-flag red if date has passed.
Bug 02
Academic chart (Chart.js) won't render
Chart.js requires the canvas element to be visible when it initialises. The Academics tab is hidden (display:none) on load. The chart script runs immediately and finds a zero-dimension canvas — it renders blank. Clicking to Academics shows an empty chart box.
Fix: trigger chart.js initialisation on tab click, not on page load.
Bug 03
Service ordering — Recommended shows above Active
Summer Camp (Recommended) appears first in the Services tab above School Matching and Tutoring which are both Active. The ordering from Notion is inconsistent. Active services should always come first.
Fix: sort services client-side: Active → Recommended → Paused.
Bug 04
Internal consultant notes exposed to parents
The Profile tab shows the raw Notion "Notes" field which currently reads: "TEST FAMILY — Do not contact. Ping targeting Bromsgrove Year 9 entry 2027." This is an internal operations note. Parents should not see operational notes meant for Satit only.
Fix: add a separate "Message from Consultant" field in Notion DB1, display only that in the portal.
· Gaps — What Parents Actually Need ·

What's Missing That Matters

A Thai parent logging into this portal for the first time has three core questions:
1. Is my child on track?  ·  2. What do I need to do right now?  ·  3. How much is this going to cost?

The portal currently answers #1 reasonably well. It doesn't answer #2 or #3 clearly enough.

CRITICAL
No "Action Required" alert for overdue or urgent items
When a parent logs in and something is overdue or due within 14 days, there is no prominent red banner at the top. The urgency is buried inside the milestone list. A parent might miss the UKISET deadline entirely. A full-width alert at the very top of Overview — "⚠️ 1 item requires your attention" — would immediately tell them what needs to happen.
CRITICAL
No cost or fees section
The portal shows the family's budget (£45,000/yr) but never shows the actual school fees. Thai parents need to see: estimated total cost of the programme (school fees + tutoring + summer camp + guardianship + flights), broken down in both GBP and Thai Baht. This is the single most anxiety-inducing question for Thai families and the portal offers nothing. Should live on the Schools tab and/or its own tab.
IMPORTANT
No academic benchmarks or context
Maths B, Science C — parents don't know if this is good or bad for a UK Year 9 entry. The academic table needs a target column ("Target: A") and a trend indicator (↑ ↓ →). Without context, grades are meaningless to a Thai parent unfamiliar with the UK grading system.
IMPORTANT
Document due dates not shown
The documents checklist shows status (Missing, Requested, Verified) but not when each document is due. The Notion database has a "Required By" date field — it's not being displayed. A parent seeing "❌ Teacher Reference 2 — Missing" doesn't know if that's due next week or next year.
IMPORTANT
No FAQ section for Thai parents
First-time UK boarding school parents have recurring anxious questions that aren't in any tab: What happens if Ping doesn't get an offer? What exactly is a UK guardian? Will there be Thai food? How does the CE exam work? How often can we visit? A simple FAQ tab would reduce repetitive LINE messages to Satit significantly — this is high leverage for Satit's time.
NICE TO HAVE
No "last synced" timestamp
Parents don't know if the data is from today or last month. A small "Last updated: 8 Mar 2026, 15:43" at the bottom of each tab builds trust and makes clear the portal is live and not static.
NICE TO HAVE
School cards show no fees or key facts
The Schools tab shows a Bromsgrove card with linked milestones and a "View School Profile" button — but no fees, boarding ratio, academic results, or scholarship info. A parent should be able to see the headline numbers directly in the portal without having to click through to the website.
